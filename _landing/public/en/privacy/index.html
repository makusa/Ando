Ando Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. Who we are

Ando is a personal finance app designed and operated by BMSK, 5025 Westmore Avenue, Montreal, Quebec H4V 1Z4, Canada ("we").

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: Régis Bile Masuke, Manager. Email: confidentialite@bmsk.ca

2. Scope

  • the website ando.bmsk.ca (beta waiting list);
  • the Ando iPhone app, in its beta version distributed through TestFlight.

3. Our principle

Your financial data stays on your device. We have no server that receives your accounts, transactions or receipts. The app contains no audience measurement, no advertising and no trackers.

4. The ando.bmsk.ca website

What we collect

Only if you join the waiting list:

InformationWhy
Your email addressTo send you the beta invitation
The page language (French or English)To write to you in your language
Your country, derived from your connection by our hostTo know where sign-ups come from
The sign-up date and the version of the consent text shownTo be able to prove your consent

We do not keep your IP address. The website sets no cookies and uses no audience measurement tool.

What we do with it

Your address is used only to send you the beta invitation, as promised on the page: no newsletter, no sharing with third parties. We will send other messages (such as the launch announcement) only if you separately agree.

Retention

Up to 12 months after the end of the beta, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Hosting and third-party services

Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website and the waiting list. This data may be stored outside Quebec, notably in the United States. The website uses no other service: the typeface is hosted on our own site.

5. The Ando app

What stays on your device

Your accounts, transactions, budgets, loans, contribution room, scanned receipts and settings are stored on your iPhone. We have no access to them. Face ID or passcode unlocking happens on the device; we receive no biometric data. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device.

Receipt text recognition and automatic suggestions (categories, recurring payments) are computed on the device, without sending anything to an external service.

iCloud sync (optional, paid tiers)

If you turn it on, your data is synced between your devices through iCloud, in your private space, under your Apple account. This service is provided by Apple Inc. under its own privacy policy; we have no access to this data.

External services contacted by the app

The app contacts a single third-party service: ExchangeRate-API, to update exchange rates. The request contains only the base currency (e.g. CAD); it contains neither your amounts, nor your transactions, nor your identity, but the service receives your connection's IP address.

To display a financial institution's logo, the app requests it from our server (ando.bmsk.ca). Like any website, it receives your IP address and the name of the institution requested, but it does not keep these requests. It obtains the logo from Logo.dev without passing on your IP address or any information about you. The logo is then kept on your device; when none exists, the app shows a monogram.

Bank connection

The beta does not offer a connection to your bank. If this feature is added, it will be optional, this policy will be updated and your consent will be requested before any connection.

Exports

When you export your transactions (CSV file), the file is created on your device and shared wherever you choose.

TestFlight beta

The beta is distributed through TestFlight, an Apple service. If we invite you by email, we know that address. If you send us feedback or a screenshot through TestFlight, we receive it. Apple may also share crash reports and technical device information with us.

Purchases

Any purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). We receive neither your payment details nor your identity; only confirmation that a purchase is active.

6. Information processed outside Quebec

The providers mentioned above (Cloudflare, Apple, ExchangeRate-API) may process information outside Quebec: in the United States for Cloudflare and Apple; in South Africa, Ireland and Germany for ExchangeRate-API. We only entrust them with the information needed for the service described in this policy.

7. Your rights

You may at any time:

  • access the information we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate;
  • withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it (for example, leave the waiting list);
  • receive your information in a structured, commonly used technological format (portability).

To exercise a right, write to the person in charge of the protection of personal information (section 1). We reply within 30 days.

Data stored in the app stays on your device: we do not hold it. You can export your transactions (CSV) from the app, and delete everything from Settings ▸ Reset All Data; if iCloud sync is on, the deletion also applies to your iCloud account and your other devices. Deleting the app erases the data on the device but not its iCloud copy, which you can delete from your device's iCloud settings.

8. Security

App data is protected by device encryption and, if you enable it, by Face ID or a passcode. Technical secrets are kept in the iOS keychain. The waiting list can only be accessed with a restricted access key.

In the event of a confidentiality incident presenting a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

9. Age

Ando is intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information about minors.

10. Changes

We will post any change on this page, with its date. If a change affects how we use your information, we will inform you and, where required, ask for your consent.

11. Questions and complaints

Please write first to the person in charge of the protection of personal information (section 1). You may also contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or, if you live elsewhere in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
