## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data?

On your iPhone. There is no server that receives your accounts,
transactions or receipts. If you turn on iCloud sync, it is also in your
private iCloud space — which we cannot access.

### Can I connect my bank?

Not yet. Automatic bank sync requires a production agreement with an account
access provider, and that agreement is not in place. In the meantime, you
can **import a statement** in `.qfx`, `.ofx`, `.csv` or `.pdf` format from
Settings ▸ Import a statement.

### What happens if I change iPhone?

If iCloud sync is on, your data follows. Otherwise it follows your iPhone
backup. **Keep one or the other on**: we cannot restore anything for you.

### I bought Pro on another device

Settings ▸ My subscription ▸ **Restore purchases**. The purchase is attached
to your Apple Account, not to the device.

### How do I cancel the Max subscription?

In your Apple Account settings, not in Ando — Apple does not let an app
cancel a subscription. iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid for.

### Do I need a passcode to open Ando?

No, it is optional. You can turn it on or off at any time in
Settings ▸ Security. Your data stays on your device either way, and your
iPhone's own lock already protects it.

### Is the contribution room shown accurate?

These are **estimates**, calculated from what you entered and from published
limits. The amount that counts is the one on your notice of assessment from
the Canada Revenue Agency, and from Revenu Québec for Québec measures. If
they differ, trust your notice of assessment, not the app.

### Why does a total show "≈"?

Because it is partial. One or more amounts are in a currency whose exchange
rate could not be retrieved: they are **excluded** from the total rather
than added as they are, which would distort the result. The number of
excluded items is shown. The total becomes complete again once rates return.

### How do I delete all my data?

Settings ▸ Danger zone ▸ **Reset all data**. Deletion is immediate and
irreversible, and extends to your other devices if iCloud sync is on.
Deleting the app also erases local data.

### What languages does Ando support?

French, English, Spanish and Portuguese. The language is set in Settings,
independently of your iPhone's language.

description lets us connect them to a real case.
